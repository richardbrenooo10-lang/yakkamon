02 / play philosophy

Three ways to stay curious.

The shape of play is still developing. These principles are the clearest map of what Yakkamon aims to feel like.

01 — NOTICE

Look past the obvious.

Creature collecting starts with observation. Distinctive silhouettes, habits, and the spaces around them should give you reasons to pause.

02 — CHOOSE

Let your collection be yours.

There is no single right way to care about a collection. Discover what catches your eye and make room for changing interests.

03 — RETURN

Revisit with new context.

A growing world gets more interesting when familiar places can reveal a different detail, creature, or route.
